## What is established (T1, verified 2026-08-06)

**Self-funding ratio: operating cash flow $1,216M ÷ capex $14.6M = 83.3×.** Capex is **0.75% of revenue**. Against hyperscalers at ~1.2–1.5× and SpaceX/xAI at 0.12×, this is the cleanest pass of the 2026-08-06 growth-quality test in the corpus.

## What is NOT established — and this is the load-bearing half

1. **That the moat is defensible.** The ontology / customer-owned-data / "you own the alpha" framing is **management's own language** (T1 Karp + T2 call), corroborated by one analyst PT note. That is confirmation of the *claim*, not of its durability.
2. **That lab competition caused the 2026 drawdown.** The documented driver was **multiple compression** (~87× forward earnings / ~40× sales inside a broad high-multiple repricing). The lab-displacement story existed as a narrative strand and was *not* the measured cause.
3. **Anything at all about the actual competitive test.** **Ode** — a **$1.5B JV of Anthropic, Blackstone, Goldman Sachs and Hellman & Friedman**, built on acquiring Fractional AI, ~100 forward-deployed engineers, thesis *"implementation, not models"* — **launched roughly one month before this quarter closed.** This print carries **essentially zero information** about it. Reading Q2 as a refutation of the commoditisation thesis is a timing error.
4. 🔴 **The bear datapoint every headline buried: revenue +93% YoY against TOTAL bookings (TCV) +49% YoY** — revenue growing ~1.9× the bookings rate. With US-commercial TCV at +153%, the remainder of the book is growing far below 49%; derived international revenue is **~+33% YoY on ~19% of mix**. That is a leading indicator on the **2027** base.
