# LLY (Eli Lilly) — Facts

Raw numerical facts only. No interpretation — see `interpretations.md`. Compiled 2026-08-08 as the first substantive research pass on the oral-vs-injectable GLP-1 question (operator holds LLY; see `thesis.md` for the honest-stub context this pass is filling).

## Efficacy — orforglipron (Lilly oral, brand Foundayo) — ATTAIN program (obesity, non-diabetic)

- **ATTAIN-1** (72 weeks, obesity/overweight, no diabetes): highest dose (36mg) mean weight loss 12.4% (27.3 lbs / 12.4kg) at 72 weeks. 59.6% of highest-dose participants lost ≥10% body weight; 39.6% lost ≥15%. [Lilly investor release, T1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-oral-glp-1-orforglipron-demonstrated-statistically); [HCPLive summary of same release, T2](https://www.hcplive.com/view/attain-1-orforglipron-achieves-up-to-12-weight-loss-in-phase-3-obesity-trial)
- **ATTAIN-1 discontinuation:** AE-driven discontinuation 5.1% (6mg) / 7.7% (12mg) / 10.3% (36mg) vs. 2.6% placebo. Overall (any-reason) discontinuation 21.9% (6mg) / 22.5% (12mg) / 24.4% (36mg) vs. 29.9% placebo. [Lilly investor release, T1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-oral-glp-1-orforglipron-demonstrated-statistically)
- **ATTAIN-2** (72 weeks, obesity/overweight WITH type 2 diabetes): highest-dose mean weight loss 10.5% (22.9 lbs), A1C reduced 1.8%. First completed long-term (72-wk) trial of an oral small-molecule GLP-1. [Lilly investor release, T1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-oral-glp-1-orforglipron-successful-third-phase-3-trial)
- **ATTAIN-MAINTAIN** (Phase 3b, 52 weeks, switch-study not head-to-head): patients previously on tirzepatide (SURMOUNT-5, n=205) or semaglutide (n=171) switched to orforglipron or placebo. Orforglipron maintained 78.0% of prior weight reduction in the tirzepatide-cohort vs. 49.8% placebo; 82.4% maintained in the semaglutide-cohort vs. 38.3% placebo. **Trial does NOT compare orforglipron against continued injectable treatment** — only against placebo, in patients already stopped from the injectable. [Nature Medicine, T1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04386-7); [Science Media Centre expert reaction, T2](https://www.sciencemediacentre.org/expert-reaction-to-an-rct-on-using-orforglipron-for-maintenance-of-body-weight-reduction-after-weight-loss-using-injectable-tirzepatide-or-semaglutide/)
- **ACHIEVE program** (orforglipron in type 2 diabetes, 5 global registrational trials, >6,000 enrolled, began 2023): results anticipated "later this year and into 2026" — full readouts not yet located as of 2026-08-08. [HCPLive summary, T2](https://www.hcplive.com/view/attain-1-orforglipron-achieves-up-to-12-weight-loss-in-phase-3-obesity-trial)
- **No head-to-head RCT exists** between orforglipron and tirzepatide or semaglutide as of 2026-08-08. All comparisons in press/analyst coverage are cross-trial. [Science Media Centre, T2](https://www.sciencemediacentre.org/expert-reaction-to-an-rct-on-using-orforglipron-for-maintenance-of-body-weight-reduction-after-weight-loss-using-injectable-tirzepatide-or-semaglutide/)

## Efficacy — tirzepatide (Lilly injectable, Zepbound/Mounjaro) — SURMOUNT-1

- 72 weeks, obesity/overweight, no diabetes: mean weight loss 16.0% (5mg) / 21.4% (10mg) / 22.5% (15mg) vs. 2.4% placebo. [Lilly investor release, T1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-surmount-1-results-published-new-england-journal-medicine)
- ≥25% body-weight reduction achieved by 15.3% (5mg) / 32.3% (10mg) / 36.2% (15mg) of participants vs. 1.5% placebo. [Lilly investor release, T1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-surmount-1-results-published-new-england-journal-medicine)
- GI-adverse-event-driven discontinuation: 1.9% (5mg) / 3.3% (10mg) / 4.3% (15mg) vs. 0.5% placebo. Overall (any-reason) discontinuation: 14.3% (5mg) / 16.4% (10mg) / 15.1% (15mg) vs. 26.4% placebo. [Wiley DOM pooled SURMOUNT-1-to-4 GI tolerability analysis, T1](https://dom-pubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/dom.16176)

## Efficacy — semaglutide (Novo) — injectable (Wegovy) vs. oral (Wegovy pill / high-dose Rybelsus)

- **STEP 1** (68 weeks, injectable 2.4mg weekly, obesity/overweight): mean weight loss 14.9% vs. 2.4% placebo. ≥5% loss in >86%; ≥10% in ~69%; ≥15% in ~51%; ≥20% in ~33%. [Wegovy trial-results page citing STEP 1, T2](https://www.wegovy.com/obesity/is-wegovy-right-for-me/wegovy-pill-results.html)
- **OASIS 4** (64 weeks, oral semaglutide 25mg daily, obesity/overweight with ≥1 comorbidity): mean weight loss 16.6% among treatment-adherent participants. ~1/3 of adherent participants lost ≥20% vs. <3% placebo. Early responders (≥10% loss by week 16, 28.8% of cohort) reached 21.6% by week 64; non-early-responders still reached 11.5%. [ACC/ Applied Clinical Trials Online summary of OASIS 4, T1/T2](https://www.acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/journal-scans/2025/09/24/16/40/oasis-4); [AllSci, T2](https://allsci.com/news/clinical-trials/novo-nordisk-strengthens-oral-obesity-push-as-semaglutide-posts-21-6-weight-loss-in-phase-iii-trial/)
- Novo states comparable weight reduction and cardiometabolic improvement between oral (25mg) and injectable semaglutide in OASIS 4 reporting — this is a **cross-trial, not head-to-head, claim**. [Applied Clinical Trials Online, T2](https://www.appliedclinicaltrialsonline.com/view/novo-nordisk-oasis-analyses-highlight-cardiometabolic-benefits-oral-semaglutide)

## Regulatory / launch dates

- FDA approved oral Wegovy (25mg semaglutide pill) **2025-12-22**; broad US availability **2026-01-05**. [Fierce Pharma, T2](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/novos-wegovy-pill-makes-us-debut-starter-dose-launching-149-month-cash-paying-patients); [GLP1Clinics, T2](https://www.glp1clinics.org/blog/fda-approves-oral-wegovy-pill-first-glp1-weight-loss-2026-06-09)
- FDA approved orforglipron (Foundayo, Lilly) **2026-04-01**; available via LillyDirect from **2026-04-06**, then broader US pharmacy/telehealth distribution. [Investor release, T1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fda-approves-lillys-foundayotm-orforglipron-only-glp-1-pill); [STAT, T2](https://www.statnews.com/2026/04/01/eli-lilly-obesity-pill-approved-orforglipron-foundayo/)
- Novo therefore had roughly a **3-month first-mover window** in the oral obesity-pill category before Lilly's oral entrant launched.

## Pricing (US, 2026)

- **Zepbound** (tirzepatide, injectable): retail list price ≈ $1,086/month. LillyDirect self-pay: $299/mo (2.5mg), $399/mo (5mg), $449/mo (7.5–15mg with on-time refill within 45 days) else $599–$1,049/mo by dose. [PeptideDeck, T2/T3](https://www.peptidedeck.com/glp/zepbound-cost); [Trimi, T3](https://trytrimi.com/blog/lillydirect-zepbound-pricing-guide)
- **Foundayo** (orforglipron, oral): commercially-insured patients as low as $25/month; self-pay from $149/month. [Investor release, T1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fda-approves-lillys-foundayotm-orforglipron-only-glp-1-pill)
- **Oral Wegovy** (semaglutide pill): list price $1,349.02/month. Cash/self-pay $149–$299/month depending on dose (1.5mg $149/mo through 2026-04-15 then $199/mo; up to $299/mo highest dose). Commercially insured as low as $25/month via savings offer. [Fierce Pharma, T2](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/novos-wegovy-pill-makes-us-debut-starter-dose-launching-149-month-cash-paying-patients)
- **Brand injectable semaglutide/tirzepatide average cash price without insurance:** ≈$997.58/month (Ozempic/Rybelsus average), ≈$1,349.02/month (Wegovy). [WeightLossRankings pricing index, T3](https://www.weightlossrankings.org/research/glp1-pricing-index)
- **Compounded (503A/503B) semaglutide/tirzepatide, 2026 post-shortage-resolution:** semaglutide $149–$299/month; tirzepatide $150–$600/month plus $40–$100 telehealth membership/consult fees. Still legal only under patient-specific 503A compounding (allergy/non-standard-dose exceptions) — the broad-availability compounding era ended with FDA shortage-resolution wind-down (tirzepatide Dec 2024, semaglutide Feb 2025). [PharmacyTimes / Trimi / IvyRx, T2/T3](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/fda-moves-to-permanently-close-the-door-on-compounded-glp-1s)
- Net: self-pay cash prices for oral (both Foundayo and oral Wegovy, $149–$299/mo entry) sit at a **discount to self-pay injectable entry tiers** ($299+/mo) at low doses, but LIST prices for injectables ($1,086–$1,349/mo) and orals ($1,349.02/mo for oral Wegovy) are comparable — Novo has NOT discounted oral Wegovy's list price versus its own injectable.

## Patents / exclusivity

- **Semaglutide** (Novo): primary US compound patent (8,129,343) filed 2007, extended to expiry **2031-12-05**. Novo holds 39+ US drug patents on semaglutide; some trackers estimate generic launch pushed to **2041** via later-filed formulation/process patents (49 total granted patents). [PharmaCompass / Trademarkia / GreyB, T2/T3](https://www.pharmacompass.com/patent-expiry-expiration/semaglutide)
- **Tirzepatide** (Lilly): key compound patent (9,474,780) filed 2015, expected US expiry **January 2036**; four active formulation patents restrict generic launch until **2039**. [GreyB / RealPeptides, T2/T3](https://greyb.com/blog/mounjaro-patent-expiration/)
- **Orforglipron** (Lilly): first core compound patent (US20190225604A1) filed 2018 — brand exclusivity expected to extend "well into the late 2030s" per patent trackers (exact expiry date not independently confirmed at T1 in this pass). [PatentVest / Synapse, T2/T3](https://www.patentvest.com/patentvest-pulse/the-oral-small-molecule-glp-1-race-beyond-orforglipron/)

## Competitor pipeline (oral GLP-1 / incretin, stage as of 2026-08-08)

- **Structure Therapeutics — aleniglipron (GSBR-1290)**, oral small molecule. Phase 2b ACCESS: placebo-adjusted weight loss 11.3% at 120mg/36wks; exploratory ACCESS II up to 15.3% at 240mg/36wks. Phase 3 initiation targeted Q3 2026 (study GSBR-1290-12). [Structure Therapeutics IR, T1](https://ir.structuretx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/structure-therapeutics-reports-positive-topline-data-phase-2)
- **Novo Nordisk — amycretin**, GLP-1/amylin dual agonist, oral AND subcutaneous formulations in development. Oral Phase 1 (12 weeks): 13.1% weight loss. Subcutaneous Phase 1b/2a (36 weeks): 22% weight loss. Phase 3 program (overweight/obesity) targeted to start Q1 2026. GlobalData estimates US approval not until **Q4 2030** (EU Q1 2031) if Phase 3 succeeds. [BioSpace / Xtalks / Fierce Biotech, T1/T2](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/novo-nordisk-to-advance-subcutaneous-and-oral-amycretin-for-weight-management-into-phase-3-clinical-development)
- Novo's **oral semaglutide (Wegovy pill / Rybelsus)** is already on-market (approved 2025-12-22) — see above.

## Market uptake, Q1–Q2 2026

- Within 3 weeks of the 2026-01-05 US oral-Wegovy launch, ~170,000 people had been prescribed a GLP-1 pill. [Search-aggregated T2 report on obesity treatment trends](https://www.ddw-online.com/obesity-treatment-trends-in-2026-and-beyond-40289-202602/)
- Analyst estimate: Foundayo (orforglipron) Q2 2026 sales ≈$146M; full-year 2026 consensus ≈$1.6–1.7B, implying ~5.4M scripts needed April–December to hit the higher figure. [Fierce Pharma, T2](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/lillys-obesity-pill-foundayo-gains-early-blockbuster-expectations-analyst-suggests-5m)
- Week-13 launch-trajectory IQVIA script counts: oral Wegovy >105,000 scripts; Foundayo 19,550 scripts; Zepbound (injectable, for reference) 59,000 scripts at its own week-13. Oral Wegovy has written >600,000 cumulative scripts YTD as of the report date. [Fierce Pharma oral-GLP-1 tracker, T2](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/oral-glp-1-tracker-launch-trajectories-lilly-foundayo-novo-wegovy-pill)
- Zepbound holds ~60% share of the US injectable obesity market (vs. Wegovy injectable). Injectables overall are estimated to retain ~80% of the US branded obesity market as of Q2 2026, with oral pills at roughly the remaining ~20% combined. [Emarketer / Fierce Pharma, T2](https://www.emarketer.com/content/eli-lilly-s-oral-glp-1-obesity-pill-gains-fda-approval--rivaling-novo-nordisk-market-share)

## Payer / access

- >90% of US commercial plans require prior authorization for GLP-1 weight-loss medications (1–3 week processing). [Forbes Health / Healthline, T2/T3](https://www.forbes.com/health/weight-loss/insurance-coverage-for-glp-1/)
- Some plans place oral and injectable formulations on different formulary tiers — reported directionally ("some plans cover oral Wegovy but place injectable Wegovy on a higher tier, or vice versa") — exact payer-by-payer tiering not independently itemized in this pass. [Forbes Health, T2/T3](https://www.forbes.com/health/weight-loss/insurance-coverage-for-glp-1/)
- Step therapy exists at some commercial plans (e.g., some Cigna plans require trial of a cheaper agent such as Contrave before GLP-1 approval). [Forbes Health, T2/T3](https://www.forbes.com/health/weight-loss/insurance-coverage-for-glp-1/)
- Under 2026 TREAT Act provisions, Wegovy (injectable) is reported as the first GLP-1 widely covered under Medicare for obesity; Foundayo is described as "increasingly appearing on 2026 formularies." Exact CMS coverage determination text not independently verified at T1 in this pass. [MedSwitcher, T2/T3](https://medswitcher.com/blog/medicare-part-d-glp1-coverage-2026-complete-guide)

## Real-world preference evidence

- **Currax survey** (n>1,000 adults with obesity): 71% said they would prefer a daily pill over a weekly injection for a prescription weight-loss medication; 73% said they would be open to non-injectable/non-GLP-1 options after GLP-1 discontinuation. Source characterized as survey/stated-preference, not revealed-preference claims data. [DDW-Online aggregation of Currax survey, T2/T3](https://www.ddw-online.com/obesity-treatment-trends-in-2026-and-beyond-40289-202602/)
- Same source reports obesity-medicine specialists (who treat only ~5–10% of eligible patients) skew toward continuing to favor injections, citing the efficacy gap. [Same source, T2/T3]

## Second-order / adjacent-market data points

- Bariatric/metabolic surgery volumes: US surgical volumes declined in 2024 and 2025 after seven consecutive years of growth (per data presented at the 2026 ASMBS meeting). Separately, metabolic-bariatric surgery volume fell ~14.4% (2022→2023) and a further ~23.0% YoY in 2024, while GLP-1 prescriptions for obesity rose ~140% (2022→2024) / ~105.6% (2022→2023). [Medscape / Harvard Chan / Mass General Brigham, T1/T2](https://www.massgeneralbrigham.org/en/about/newsroom/press-releases/study-finds-bariatric-surgery-declined-with-rise-in-glp-1-drugs)
- Gastric band/balloon device market: $49.1M (2025) est., projected to $34.2M by 2035 (CAGR ≈ −3.54%). [Medical Device Network, T2](https://www.medicaldevice-network.com/analyst-comment/glp-1-medications-reduced-bariatric-surgeries/)
- CPG/food impact: GLP-1 use estimated to reduce US CPG sales by ~$32B annually, projected to ~$44B as the US GLP-1 user base grows from ~9.5M to ~12M. Restaurant dinner-hour traffic down 6% among regular GLP-1 users (~0.4% aggregate restaurant sales impact); overall restaurant spend down ~8% within first 6 months of GLP-1 use. Snack/confectionery category sales down 12.4%. GLP-1 users report 21% fewer calories consumed and ~1/3 lower grocery spend (KPMG). [FTI Consulting / mealfan.com aggregation / KPMG-cited, T2/T3](https://mealfan.com/glp-1-restaurant-cpg-impact-2026/)
- Telehealth GLP-1 distribution: Hims & Hers posted a $92M Q1 2026 loss tied to its pivot from compounded to branded GLP-1 sales (following March 2026 FDA warning letters); signed a partnership with Novo Nordisk to distribute Ozempic/Wegovy exclusively as a branded distribution partner, ceasing compounded-GLP-1 promotion. FY2026 revenue guidance raised to $2.8–3.0B (+19–28% YoY). [STAT / FierceHealthcare, T1/T2](https://www.statnews.com/2026/02/23/him-hers-earnings-2026-outlook-compounded-semaglutide/)
- Pen/autoinjector device manufacturers in the GLP-1 supply chain include Ypsomed (UnoPen, YpsoMate, ServoPen), SHL Medical (Molly), and Phillips-Medisize (Envoi). No company-specific 2026 financial data on oral-substitution impact was found in this pass — flagged as a gap. [Ypsomed/SHL/Phillips-Medisize product pages, T3](https://www.ypsomed.com/en/products/pen-injectors)
