## §1 — What was actually ordered

**Governor Abbott, letter dated 2026-08-03** to the PUCT chair and ERCOT CEO ([gov.texas.gov](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-directs-comprehensive-data-center-audit), T1). **A directive letter, not an executive order** — no EO number exists.

Abbott's own framing: *"no more additional data centers can be approved or move forward"* until the audit completes; any non-compliant project *"must be denied connection to the Texas grid."* Required per project: tax incentives received, power demand and self-generation, water volume/source/cooling, community-impact mitigation, and **facility ownership**.

🟢 **The fact that turns "audit" into a de facto moratorium: ERCOT suspended Batch Zero.** It will **not issue the 2026-08-07 project classifications** (base load / studied load / excluded) and will seek a timeline exception at the **PUCT open meeting on 2026-08-20**. That is a hard, dated stoppage of the single gating milestone in Texas large-load interconnection.

**So: "moratorium" is defensible in effect, imprecise in form.** Carry it as *"audit-triggered suspension of the Batch Zero interconnection process."* **No end date is published.**

**Not a recycle, and note the distinction from the earlier action:** Abbott issued a *separate* directive on **2026-06-10** requiring data centres to fully fund their own interconnection so costs don't hit residential ratepayers. The 08-03 action is an escalation, not a re-announcement.
