## 2. THE BRIDGE — this is a DEPRECIATION story, not a demand story

Two disclosed Q1 lines give the load-bearing number (all T1, reported 2026-05-12 via Nikkei disclosure / Kabutan 決算速報):

| Q1 CY2026 (Jan-Mar) | Reported |
|---|---|
| Net sales | **¥101,402mn** (−1.0% YoY) |
| Operating profit | **−¥5,273mn** |
| Ordinary profit | **−¥7.9bn** |
| Net income (attributable) | **−¥8,469mn** |
| EPS | **−¥24.22** vs company's own forecast **−¥14.73** (missed by 64.4%) |
| **EBITDA** | **¥23.4bn — 23.1% margin** |

**Derivation (my calc from the two cited lines):**

> **D&A = EBITDA − OP = ¥23.4bn − (−¥5.273bn) = ¥28.67bn per quarter**

≈ **28.3% of quarterly revenue** (¥28.67bn ÷ ¥101.402bn, my calc) consumed by depreciation — the Imari/Saga programme (¥201.5bn: ¥78.6bn building/utility + ¥122.9bn equipment) hitting the P&L.

**SUMCO is solidly EBITDA-positive and only OP-negative because of new-fab depreciation.** Any read of the loss as demand weakness is reading the wrong line. This is the single most important framing correction in this file.

### The forecast identity

> **Q2 OP = (Revenue × EBITDA margin) − D&A**

**Validating it against the company's own guide** (¥112bn sales / −¥2.5bn OP, issued 2026-05-12):
- ¥112bn × 23.1% (Q1 margin) = **¥25.872bn** EBITDA
- ¥25.872bn − ¥28.67bn D&A = **−¥2.798bn**
- Guide is −¥2.5bn → the guide implies EBITDA of **¥26.17bn = 23.37% margin** (my calc: (−¥2.5bn + ¥28.67bn) ÷ ¥112bn)

**The guide therefore embeds two testable assumptions:**
1. **Revenue +10.45% QoQ** (¥101.402bn → ¥112bn, my calc)
2. **EBITDA margin expanding ~27bp** (23.1% → 23.37%)

**Component-level honesty note (INPUT-layer gap, declared not hidden):** SUMCO does **NOT** publicly disclose 300mm vs 200/150mm splits, shipment area (万c㎡), or polished/epi/SOI mix — exhaustive search incl. Japanese-language primary returned NOT DISCLOSED on all three; 決算説明会資料 PDFs were unreachable. **A literal volume × ASP × LTA-mix build is therefore not constructible from public data, and I will not fabricate one.** The EBITDA-minus-D&A identity above is the highest-resolution bottoms-up model the disclosure permits, and it has the advantage of being *checkable against the guide*, which a made-up component split would not be.
